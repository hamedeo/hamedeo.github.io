<div class="about-block">
  <h2 class="about-section-heading">My Life</h2>
  <section class="about-section">

Born three times, in Iran, Italy, and the Netherlands, I have built seven years of experience delivering solutions in engineering and project management. 

My professional journey began with the successful delivery of several projects in the IT domain over three years. I then started to work as a mechanical consultant for a year and a half, supporting the development of pressure vessels for a major steel manufacturer in Iran, **Mobarakeh Steel Company**.

The joy of solving complexity in motion drew me toward a master’s degree in mechanical engineering at Polytechnic of Turin. There, I deepened my technical foundation and prepared for my next chapter at Eindhoven University of Technology, where I conducted research on powder production analysis for metal additive manufacturing.

I later joined **ASML**, a global leader in the semiconductor industry, where I learned that the highest levels of technical complexity can only be achieved through teamwork, collaboration, and shared purpose across borders.

A year later, I founded my own consultancy, **Morpheidos Tech**, driven by the wish to turn technical know-how into meaningful solutions. Yet I came to understand that delivery without reflection is incomplete. After supporting some projects, I decided to strengthen the human skills that had once been secondary in my professional life.

That decision led me to the **Digital Society School** at Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences, where I stepped beyond my comfort zone and gained new insights into the meaning of teamwork, the art of communication, and the continuous pleasure of creation.

Because creation is, in many ways, life’s growing light, a redemption from suffering, a discipline of change, and a willingness to be transformed by what we choose to build.
  </section>
