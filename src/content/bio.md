I am a **Mechanical Engineer** with background in thermo-fluids engineering and precision hardware. I enjoy technical analysis and support mechanical development from A to Z where design initiates, validation takes place and solutions seems to be not working. I have experience working for companies like ASML, Mobarakeh Steel Engineering, various engineering consultancies and academic environment such as Polytechnic of Turin & TU Eindhoven.

## My Life

Born three times, in Iran, Italy, and the Netherlands, I have built seven years of experience across mechanical engineering, project management in thermofluids engineering and precision hardware.

My professional journey began with the successful delivery of several projects in the IT domain. I then started to work as a mechanical consultant for a year and a half, supporting the development of pressure vessels for a major steel manufacturer in Isfahan, Iran.

The joy of solving complexity in motion drew me toward a master’s degree in mechanical engineering at one of Italy’s leading universities. There, I built technical depth and prepared for my next chapter in conducting research at Eindhoven University of Technology in the Netherlands, modelling powder production for metal additive manufacturing.

I later joined ASML, a global leader in the semiconductor industry, where I learned that the highest levels of technical complexity can only be achieved through teamwork, collaboration, and shared purpose across borders.

A year later, I founded my own consultancy, Morpheidos Tech, driven by the dream of delivering meaningful solutions through technical complexity. Yet I came to understand that delivery without reflection  (without learning) is incomplete. After supporting some projects, I decided to strengthen the human skills that had once been secondary in my professional life.

That decision led me to the Digital Society School at Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences, where I stepped beyond my comfort zone and gained new insights into the meaning of teamwork, the art of communication, and the continuous pleasure of creation.

Because creation is, in many ways, life’s growing light, a redemption from suffering, a discipline of change, and a willingness to be transformed by what we choose to build.
